# UK Tax Check

Free, no-signup estimate of possible UK Income Tax and National Insurance for employees and self-employed people (sole trader, CIS, taxi/delivery, locum, online seller).

Open [index.html](index.html) or use GitHub Pages after you enable it.

**Not tax advice.** Estimates only — check figures on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/check-income-tax-current-year).

## What’s included
- Guided questions (England, Wales, Northern Ireland rates)
- Allowable expenses, mileage, trading allowance, capital allowances
- Year-to-date projection and optional “already paid” comparison

## Source
Multi-file source is under `src/` for development. The live page is the single-file `index.html`.
